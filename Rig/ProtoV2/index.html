Proto-Dragon V2 Rig

Each part is filled with a flat colour. The black-and-white layer art sits on top as ink: white lets the colour through, black stays solid, greys shade it.

Colours

Rock dragon: Light shade colours the cracks and belly, Dark shade colours the spikes and horns. It keeps its own palette, so your colours for the other versions are untouched.

Refinement pass

Outline corners that doubled up into an L are trimmed so lines step diagonally at an even 1px weight, lone specks of the wrong shade are filled in, the stray near-black grey is merged into the outline shade, and a faint pattern of scales is added to the body, head, legs and tail in a light grey that shows as a slightly deeper shade of the chosen colour. Shapes, shading and pivots are unchanged.
